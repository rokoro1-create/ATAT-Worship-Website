Our Story

Born from a
divine appointment

A Time As This Global is more than a worship ministry — it is a calling. Rooted in the courage of Esther, we believe every generation is appointed for a specific moment in God's redemptive story.

"Who knows whether you have not come to the kingdom for such a time as this?"

We are a borderless, online-first ministry — gathering believers from every nation, tongue, and time zone to lift up the name of Jesus through anointed music, heartfelt prayer, and the unashamed proclamation of His word. No walls. No limits. Just worship.

What We Believe

The Pillars of
Our Worship

01

Presence

We pursue the manifest presence of God above all else. Our worship is not performance — it is pursuit.

02

Spirit & Fire

Anointed worship ignites transformation. We welcome the Holy Spirit to move freely in every gathering.

03

Purpose

Like Esther, we are not here by accident. Every voice, every instrument, every moment is sovereignly appointed.

04

Excellence

We offer God our very best — skilled musicianship, heartfelt devotion, and a spirit of excellence in all we do.

05

Community

Worship is not a solo endeavour. We are one body, one voice, lifting one name together in unity and love.

06

Kingdom

Our worship points beyond this room to the King of Kings — advancing His kingdom one song, one prayer at a time.
